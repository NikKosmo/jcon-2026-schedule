# JCON Europe 2026 — Schedule & Alternatives

Personal working schedule for [JCON Europe 2026](https://2026.europe.jcon.one/), held 20–23 April 2026 at the Cinedom Multiplex Cinema in Cologne.

Each day file contains the full session list plus an **Online Alternative** column — pre-researched direct YouTube recordings, slides, articles, and GitHub repos that cover the same topic, usually by the same speaker. When two parallel sessions conflict, the alternatives column lets you pick one live without losing access to the other.

## Conventions

- **⭐** next to a room marks a **Premium AI Track** session (Cinema 7 on Tue & Wed).
- **Online Alternative** links are strict:
  - **YouTube** is always a direct `youtube.com` / `youtu.be` URL (no wrappers, no aggregators).
  - **Article** links are substantive technical content — verified via fetch, ≥400 words of real coverage. Announcement posts and speaker profile pages are rejected.
  - Priority: same-speaker, same-topic recording first; fall back to same-speaker adjacent topic; then a good article by the speaker.
  - A dash (`-`) means nothing good enough surfaced — better no alternative than a misleading one.
- Source schedule captured 2026-04-17 — JCON may still publish updates before the event.
